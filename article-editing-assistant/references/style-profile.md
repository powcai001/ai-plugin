# Style Profile

Use this reference to preserve the user's writing voice when turning Chinese voice drafts into articles.

## 我的写作偏好

以下偏好在诊断时优先参考。每次用户说「记下来」就会追加到这里。

- 结尾偏好：一个判断句或短画面收束，不要温和建议，不要鸡汤，不要价值拔高。
- 开头：2-3 段内必须进入正题或冲突，不要反复铺垫同一层意思。
- 不喜欢用「科学研究表明」「研究表明」这类模糊引用来支撑观点——要么具体引用，要么删掉。
- 短文（300-800 字）保持流动，不强行加章节标题或分割线。
- 段落末尾尽量驱动下一段，段落之间要有推力，不要各自独立收束。
- 文章的中段或高潮处不需要「附带说明」打断叙事节奏。
- 转折处偏好身体感受或具体动作（僵住、愣住、放下手机），而非抽象认知（「我意识到」「我发现」）。
- 标题要有钩子（反常识、悬念、断言），不要写成一句话总结。
- 系列章节发布前：先核查对应代码，必要时修复并运行测试；但面向知乎、公众号的正文不写测试用例、测试数量或测试过程。
- AI 编辑边界：默认让 AI 严格诊断、提出问题并引导自改，不直接重写全文；只有明确要求成稿时才代为润色。
- 思考训练：观点文需要检查最强反方和适用边界，避免每篇文章都过早落回既有口号。

## Voice

- Reflective, direct, and conversational.
- Starts from something recently watched, read, tried, or noticed.
- Looks for the underlying logic behind people, tools, markets, self-discipline, productivity, and personal experience.
- Keeps first-person observation, especially phrases such as "我觉得", "给我的感觉是", "我想到", and "其实".
- Allows moderate subjective judgment instead of sanding everything into neutral commentary.

## Structure

Use whatever shape fits the piece. Common useful shapes:

- Event or trigger -> main impression -> one or two examples -> concise reflection.
- Contrast: old way vs new way, expectation vs reality, problem vs solution.
- Parallel points under one theme.
- Short opinion: one judgment plus the reason it matters.
- Story (短文推荐): 让读者感觉「出事了」——包含背景、冲突、行动、细节、收获五个要素。不是固定弧线。

For daily review or free-form brainstorm voice notes, choose only useful sections from:

- 今日概览
- 做了什么
- 重要想法
- 问题或卡点
- 明天可以推进的事
- 一句话总结

Use these only when the raw draft contains enough material. Otherwise, keep it short.

## Keep

- Concrete examples from the transcript.
- Personal side paths when they show how the user thinks.
- Mild spoken rhythm.
- Direct causal language such as "所以", "这也说明", "给我的感觉".
- Short, direct sentences when the idea is simple.

## Remove Or Compress

- Filler sounds: "呃", "嗯", "啊".
- Repeated connectors: "然后", "就是", "对吧".
- Duplicate self-corrections.
- Half-started clauses that add no meaning.
- Repeated explanation of the same point.
- Generic transitions that make the piece longer without adding meaning.
- Endings that repeat the opening.

## Avoid

- Generic inspirational endings.
- Overly formal essay style.
- Marketing headlines.
- Excessive idioms.
- Invented background details.
- Changing the user's stance to make it sound more balanced.
- Forcing a fixed structure when the article only needs a simpler shape.

## Example Transformation

Raw:

"然后其实我觉得这个这种节目，就是能看到不一样的，从媒体上不一样的人，就是比如王晶，对吧？"

Edited:

"这类访谈有意思的地方，是能看到一个人在媒体形象之外的另一面。比如王晶，他在节目里呈现出来的逻辑，和很多人想象中的电影人不太一样。"
